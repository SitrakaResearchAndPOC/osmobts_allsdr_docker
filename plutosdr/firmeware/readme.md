# For PlutoSDR 
* Connect the two usb of PlutoSDR on the computer (only one which open the conf PlutoSDR is needed but if if you don't know it's quite quick)
* Download the firmeware [here](https://github.com/SitrakaResearchAndPOC/osmobts_allsdr_docker/blob/main/plutosdr/firmeware/plutosdr-fw-v0.38_with_timestamping.zip)
* unzip it and copy pluto.frm on PlutoSDR (near the conf)
* Eject PlutoSDR and wait the flashing; it will boot automatically

# For Fishball_Zynq7020 or plutoSKY
* Unplug the SD card of PlutoSKY / Fishball , and plug it to your computer using SD card adapter
* Cut (if you want save) or erase all files at the SD card  
* Download the firmeware [here](https://github.com/SitrakaResearchAndPOC/osmobts_allsdr_docker/blob/main/plutosdr/firmeware/PlutoSky_SDR_Zynq7020_timestamping.zip) unzip it and copy all files at the SD card
* Eject the SD card and plug it at the plutoSKY / Fishball
